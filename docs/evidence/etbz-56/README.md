# ETBZ-56 — the accepted Skill reading as a PDF, through the one projection and renderer

The real accepted Skill output, presented: the ETBZ-57 reading of the synthetic known-time
chart ("Musterkundin A", no real birth data, a development fixture - not the Golden case,
Rebaseline 62128133 section 18), written under Skill and bundle 1.1.0 and accepted at the
ETBZ-57 Human Editorial Gate (Jira ETBZ-57 comment 16969), projected by
`buildSkillReadingProjection` onto the one template `bazodiac-final-template@1.0.0` and
drawn by the same `bazodiac-pdf-renderer@1.0.0` as the ETBZ-55 evidence. Decision record:
ADR 0014.

| `pages/*.png` | fifteen full-size page renders of the same run: cover, identity, glance, four pillars, foundation, day master, Wu Xing, five phases, Ten Gods, hidden stems, chapter 1 opener and continuation, reflection, summary, method note | the renderer (digests in `qa-report.json`) |
| `visual-verdict.json` | the Delivery Runner's visual evidence verdict (Rebaseline section 18): `VISUALLY_FIT_FOR_GOLDEN`, no defect, five observations, bound to the artifact and render digests | the Delivery Runner, after inspecting the renders |

`tests/contract/etbz56-skill-presentation-evidence.contract.test.ts` re-derives in CI what
needs no browser: the projection from the accepted reading, its input package and the
chart; the PDF, contact sheet and projection-file digests; the manifest's Skill identities
against the released bundle hash and the accepted reading's hash; the renderer digest
against the committed sources, equal to the ETBZ-55 manifest's and to the ETBZ-55 canary
record's (one renderer, its gates proven once); every page QA-clean; every committed render
the one the QA report hashed; the verdict bound to the same artifact.

The generation record is the operator's declaration (`declared: true`): the render ran on
the head it names, which the contract suite requires in the tested history. A second
render process produced the same PDF bytes - also a declaration; CI re-derives the
in-process DETERMINISM check the manifest records.

## What the render shows

- The reading the Human Editorial Gate accepted, word for word: seven chapters of two
  pages each, three reflection questions and the method note, set by the same paginator -
  German quotation marks included (ADR 0012 limitation 12 is resolved, ADR 0014 section 4).
- The chart the reading was written about: every fact of the input package equals the chart
  value at its path before a page is built; the pages draw the validated chart.
- Every structured display of an Earthly Branch with its Hanzi, its canonical pinyin and its
  German animal label from `bazodiac-branch-animal-labels@1.0.0` (Rebaseline 62128133 section 20) -
  glance, four pillars, foundation, day master, all twelve on the five-phases page, hidden
  stems, reflection and summary. Branches the
  reading's own text names ("Wu (午)", "Hai (亥)" and "Wei (未)" on page 12, "Wu (午)" on page 14) stay as the Skill wrote them: the
  mapper adds no word to an accepted reading, as section 20 requires (ADR 0014 section 3).
- No internal identifier on any page: the identities live in `sources`, which no page
  prints, and the PDF's text layer is bound string for string to the pages' printed
  strings (32,195 text units, 1,632 strings).

## What it does not show

- The Day-Master reading slot: the reading binds a spec to `dayMaster.reading`, which the
  template leaves empty (`NO_APPROVED_CONTENT`); the binding is recorded, nothing is filled.
- The dominant Wu Xing phase: two specs cite it; no page shows it (ADR 0012 section 4).
- A pull quote, a key insight or a motif component: the Skill reading has none, and the
  template defines none for Skill output (ETBZ-43).
- Whether the document is sellable - the human verdict of ETBZ-54.
