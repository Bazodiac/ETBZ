## What the run shows

- **Same meaning (AC 5).** The 1.1.0 reading keeps the 1.0.0 reading's structure paragraph for paragraph and changes only text: the semantic-binding projection (claim graph, chapters, paragraph kinds and postures, fact and claim references, deltas, callbacks, reflection bindings, warnings, visual specs) is equal. Only the identities differ: skill, bundle, package, plan hash and contract set.
- **New voice (AC 6, 7).** As counted by the 1.1 phrase lists (`evals.json`, `A_fixtureOldVsNew`): meta-narration 71 → 0 occurrences (35 → 0 surfaces), retired template hedges 34 → 0 (14 → 0 of 32 SUPPORTED paragraphs), tentative markers over SUPPORTED claims 7 → 0, alternatives the graph does not carry 11 → 0, tension softeners 7 → 0, life-domain words 0 → 0. The lists are closed; what they do not list is not counted.
- **Tentative stays visible (eval B).** With the Day Master fact made provisional (synthetic variant), the reading is refused as certain (`READING_PROVISIONALITY_LAUNDERED`), refused as tentative without a marker (`READING_TENTATIVE_NOT_VISIBLE`) and accepted with one.
- **Edge without drama (evals C, D; AC 13).** Tension words stand in seven paragraphs, each citing both poles of C2–C6 or C2–C1, and no softener remains; the C3–C5 contrast (chapter 5) is stated as a contrast without a tension word. A tension sentence added to the chapter whose only claim is in no contrast is refused (`READING_TENSION_UNGROUNDED`). Whether a tension is resolved into harmony is not measured by any instrument; the editorial reviews and the Human Editorial Gate judge it.
- **Individuality unchanged (evals E, F, G; AC 11).** Claim graph and plan content are identical across versions, so the graph-level anti-boilerplate steps have the same outcome by construction; the claim graph fails the swap against the unknown-time foil. Under a fact mutation (dominant phase Feuer → Holz) and a fact removal (the day branch's hidden Ten God), refusals stay inside the dependency cone for both versions; the 1.1.0 prose depends on the mutated fact in more paragraphs (7 of 9 in the cone, 1.0.0: 2 of 9).
- **Editorial pass (AC 12).** The EDIT revision is accepted as text-only; revisions that add a fact reference, a claim, a paragraph or drop a visual spec are refused (`READING_EDITORIAL_EXPANSION`); injected symbols, numbers, life domains, diagnosis, causality, deterministic identity, fate and unapproved methods are refused by their gates.

## Independent editorial review

Four reviewer instances that did not write the text labelled every surface of old and new with the closed customer-voice categories (Lens 1.1 section 12.4) and listed every new sentence that says more than its claims license; flagged sentences were checked adversarially. Round 1 found real overreach the mechanical gates cannot see: illustrations written as the reader's behaviour, feelings or abilities, claims about how others see the reader, tensions resolved into harmony, uniqueness and centrality claims (12 of 54 surfaces worse than the old text). The contract candidates were made operational (Lens 1.1 sections 9 and 9.2, Lexicon 1.1 rule 13, SKILL.md laws 9 and 10), and the reading was realised again (REALISE v2, EDIT v2). Round 2 rated 1 of 54 surfaces worse than the old text and confirmed 8 of 20 flagged sentences as overreach — most of them sentences added only to reach the 600-word chapter floor (strength of hidden stems, foreground/background ranking, content of a claim the paragraph does not cite, presupposing questions). REALISE v3 and EDIT v3 removed all 8 and reworked the 12 refuted ones. The delta review of PR #18 (round 3) then confirmed what the authors missed: v3 stated counts the chart does not carry — the controlling and the resourcing voice stand in the year, month and hour branches, not "an zwei Stellen" — and 3 more fill sentences overreached. REALISE v4 and EDIT v4 (committed in 84f1234) named positions instead of counting, replaced the 3 sentences, and a listed count word became a refusal (`READING_UNCITED_NUMERAL`). The fix verification then read the remaining position statements as complete: claims C1 and C2 name the year and the month branch, while the chart carries the same relations in the hour branch too, which a reading cannot cite there. REALISE v5 and EDIT v5 (committed in cab9ff0) came from a systematic sweep over every position and count sentence, not from the reviewers' list, and the method note now discloses that named positions are the ones an interpretation rests on. The final verification (round 5) still found three position lists without a non-exhaustive marker and new classes: statements about how contrasted claims interact ("keine hebt die andere auf" — neither that one cancels the other nor that it does not is licensed), a strength verdict ("keine laute Stimme"), the Wu-Xing weighting read as a count of the eight characters, and the demand called "von innen". REALISE v6 and EDIT v6 (this fixture) remove them. Count forms the closed list does not match ("beide Male", "drei Beziehungen") are left to the human reader. Each sentence is meant to be licensed by its paragraph's own claims and facts, a first-use explanation of a BaZi term, or a situation framed as a situation — an intention five review rounds show is not self-certifying; the Human Editorial Gate is the check.

## What it does not show

Whether any sentence is true of a person; that the chapter floor of 600 words (ETBZ-43) suits a reading with six claims — the reviewers rate the fill it forces as generic or repetitive, and removing overreach repeatedly pushed chapters below it; whether a customer prefers the new voice (that is the Human Editorial Gate); the prose-level anti-boilerplate steps 6.1 (blind attribution) and 6.7 (reuse), which need a second reading under the same version and run in ETBZ-54. The fixture reading was written by the Delivery Runner's Claude runtime, which also wrote the gates; the independent customer-voice review and the Product Owner's verdict are the checks on that.
