## 1. Main finding

No gate in code requires any hedge or any meta-language phrase. `acceptSkillReading` never demands a phrase. The over-cautious, technical voice is produced by **instructions and bundle values only**: the Lens posture column (section 2) and preferred-language list (section 9.2), the Lexicon's uncertainty table (section 7) and global rules L3.3/L3.4, and the Skill's laws 5–6 and writing procedure, which quote fixed German formulas ("die Quelle führt …", "kann als … gelesen werden", "Vielleicht erkennst du …"). One code path permits hedging a SUPPORTED claim down (C4 below). The fixture shows the result: all seven FACT paragraphs contain "Quelle"; 14 of 25 INTERPRETATION paragraphs carry the narrow hedge set ("gelesen werden", "Lesart", "Innerhalb dieses BaZi-Rahmens"); 5 of 7 REFLECTION paragraphs open with "Vielleicht", although all six claims are `SUPPORTED_INTERPRETATION` and no fact is provisional.

## 4. Safety and semantic boundaries that survive unchanged

These are real boundaries, not voice. Enforcement: **M** mechanical in code, **P** partly, **I** instructed only, **NR** defined but never run on `main`.

- **Symbolic truth (M).** FuFirE owns facts; the Skill never calculates, corrects, completes or infers a fact (SKILL law 1; Method Profile section 2; PD-7; Lens sections 6 and 13.1). `READING_FACT_UNKNOWN`, `READING_FACT_EXCLUDED`, `READING_UNCITED_SYMBOL`, `READING_UNCITED_NUMERAL`, `READING_FACT_NOT_GROUNDED`, `CLAIM_UNGROUNDED`.
- **Method permission (M).** Method Profile sections 4–7, GR-1, the deferred/forbidden list; Lens section 19; SKILL law 2. `READING_UNSUPPORTED_METHOD_LANGUAGE`, `CLAIM_METHOD_NOT_APPROVED`, `CLAIM_MODIFIER_ALONE`, `REGISTRY_NOT_RELEASED`.
- **Provisionality lineage (M on claims, posture tag only on prose).** Method Profile UT-1…UT-7, PD-10, section 8; Long-Form section 21 AC 9; SKILL laws 3 and 10; Lexicon section 7 preamble. `CLAIM_PROVISIONAL_LAUNDERED`, `READING_PROVISIONALITY_LAUNDERED`.
- **Meaning lives in the graph and plan (M).** SKILL laws 3–4, `newClaimRelationsForbidden`, `epistemicClassesFixed`, PD-5, Lens section 7.2 ("A contrast may only be written when both poles have distinct grounding"). `READING_CLAIM_*`, `READING_CHAPTER_*`, `READING_THESIS_UNRENDERED`, `PLAN_TENSION_*`.
- **No diagnosis, determinism, advice or mystification (P).** Lens sections 1, 4.2, 9.1, 9.2 avoid list, 10, 11; Lexicon sections 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12. `READING_PROHIBITED_WORDING`; the rest is read by a human.
- **Evidence off the customer surface (M).** SKILL law 9; Long-Form sections 4.5, 18; 72056833 section 8. `READING_EVIDENCE_CHROME`, `projectCustomerReading`.
- **Warnings never dropped (M).** Lexicon section 9; Method Profile section 6.2. `READING_WARNINGS_NOT_VERBATIM`.
- **Anti-Barnum / individuality (NR).** Lens sections 4.1, 12, 21; Lexicon L3.9, section 14; 72056833 sections 1–6; SKILL law 7. `individuality-contract.ts` executes no check; ETBZ-52 README: checks 6.1–6.7 were not run.

## 5. Contradictions found

- **C1 — SUPPORTED hedged by the Lexicon; Rebaseline says direct.** Lexicon section 7 prefers „Innerhalb dieses BaZi-Rahmens kann dies als … gelesen werden.“ for a supported interpretation; Rebaseline section 17: SUPPORTED interpretations may be direct and must not rely on permanent hedging. The Rebaseline wins; the Lexicon's own precedence clause blocks v1 voice execution until a new identity exists.
- **C2 — The Lens keys posture to composition level, not to claim support.** Section 2's posture column follows E1–E5; the Lens never names SUPPORTED or TENTATIVE; section 21 step 8 makes "a bounded reflective hypothesis with … alternative manifestations" a precondition for every sentence, while section 7.6 requires alternatives to be evidence-grounded.
- **C3 — SKILL.md contradicts itself.** Law 6 applies bounded formulations to all interpretation; law 3 renders a claim "with its own epistemic class"; procedure step 4 sets posture "as the claims require".
- **C4 — The boundary allows hedging down.** `skill-reading.ts:481` accepts an INTERPRETATION or REFLECTION paragraph over SUPPORTED-only claims tagged TENTATIVE; the plan constraint (`meta-narrative-plan.ts:230`) says a claim is rendered with its own class.
- **C5 — Method Profile section 8 permits uncertainty to increase downstream; DIRECTNESS points the other way.** Not a normative contradiction: section 8 grants a permission and does not require added uncertainty; customer wording is not a Method Profile domain. The revision narrows the permission below the Method Profile: uncertainty is **carried, not added** — it increases only structurally (a TENTATIVE class, a provisional fact, a QUALIFIES or ALTERNATIVE_READING relation). **The Product Owner confirms this reading at the 57.5 gate.**
- **C6 — The Skill writes facts as "die Quelle führt …"; the Lexicon owns „Dein Chart zeigt …“**, and ETBZ-57 AC 7 names "die Quelle führt" as meta-language.
- **C7 — The Lexicon forbids a phrase bank (L3.8, section 14) and supplies one (section 7 templates; SKILL.md quotes them).** Fixture: "Innerhalb dieses BaZi-Rahmens" 9 times.
- **C8 — The Lexicon wants the four kinds distinguishable "in language"; the customer projection strips `kind` and `posture`.** Only wording then carries the difference, which pushes toward verbal signposting.
- **C9 — Alternatives must be licensed (Lens section 7.6, Lexicon section 4) but nothing ties them to `ALTERNATIVE_READING`.** Fixture: 5 "andere mögliche Ausdrucksform" sentences, 0 such relations.
- **C10 — Content should shrink rather than pad (Method Profile section 10; Rebaseline section 5) but chapters have a 600-word floor** (`CHAPTER_WORD_BUDGET`, owned by ETBZ-43's visual contract). Out of this slice; the ETBZ-52 README records chapters refused as too short and lengthened.
- **C11 — The Lexicon's AUTHORITY wording contains "Verantwortung", which is a Ten-God `labelDe`**; `READING_UNCITED_SYMBOL` refuses it unless a cited fact carries it.
- **C12 — Lexicon section 12 bans "always"/"never" without context; the code deliberately does not.** The revision qualifies the ban to deterministic statements about the person.
- **C13 — Phrase gates are over-broad in places** ("zu viel", "du brauchst" match anywhere) **and blind to negation and paraphrase.** Recorded; scoping a safety gate is out of this slice.
- **C14 — Several sources ask for "psychologically meaningful / articulate / legible" prose; Rebaseline section 17 asks for "BaZi-authentisch statt pseudo-psychologisch".** The revision defines "legible" as everyday language, not psychological taxonomy.
- **C15 — Unbound ambiguity (Rebaseline section 5, Lens section 1 goal 4) vs the rainbow-statement ban (Lens section 12.2) and INTERPRETIVE_EDGE.** Fixture: „Welche dieser Formen zutrifft, kann das Chart nicht sagen.“ The revision binds ambivalence to `CONTRASTS_WITH` and `ALTERNATIVE_READING`.
- **C16 — Month-branch wording: Method Profile section 7 "principal Qi of your month branch" vs Lexicon "Monatsreferenz (Yue Ling)".** Recorded for the Lexicon revision; no method change.
- **C17 — A surface cleanup must not remove BaZi anchors.** 72056833 section 6.1 needs a reader to attribute a reading from its named anchors; only provenance moves below the surface, symbols stay in the narrative.
