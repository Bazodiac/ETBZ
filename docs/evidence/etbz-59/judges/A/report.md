## Blind attribution for check 6.1: Reading X is chart 3 (PASS), Reading Y is chart 2 (PASS)

The two near neighbours are charts 2 and 3. Their year, month and day pillars and their day master are identical. They differ only in the hour pillar (line 4) and the Wu Xing weights (line 6). Each reading cites both the hour pillar and the weights, so each separates the two neighbours on raw symbols and numbers.

The readings use German relation names and the charts use English Ten God labels. I took "Disruptive Ausgabe" as HurtingOfficer, "Druck / Struktur" as SevenKilling and "Indirekte Quelle" as IndirectRes. That mapping is my inference from the readings' own definitions, which give each relation's direction to the day master and its variant. None of the deciding anchors below depends on it; they are stem names, branch names, hidden stems and weight values.

I used only the Read tool on the five files. I made no other call.
