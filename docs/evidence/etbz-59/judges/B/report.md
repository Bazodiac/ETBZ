## 6.7 Cross-reading reuse scan (S/N)

## N [2.4]

**Verdict: N_UNGROUNDED_GENERALISATION (outside the contract's codes). The sentence is false for N on the family reading and ungrounded on any reading.**
- **Chart line** (`chart-near.txt`): "hour: stem Bing 丙 (Feuer), branch Shen 申 (Affe); visible Ten God DirectOfficer; hidden Geng (RobWealth), Ren (HurtingOfficer), Wu (DirectRes)"
- **N's own definition in [2.1]:** "Eine Phase, die den Tagesmeister kontrolliert, gehört in der BaZi-Tradition zur Familie von Struktur und Anforderung" and "Die BaZi-Tradition kennt in dieser Familie auch eine geregeltere Form". N [0.0] lists Bing as a visible stem: "die Stämme Geng, Ren, Xin und Bing".
- **Family reading:** in standard BaZi, DirectOfficer is the regulated member of that controlling family. So the requirement does appear on the surface of the hour pillar, and the sentence is false.
- **Narrow reading ("Druck / Struktur" only):** true for the chart, since no SevenKilling stem is visible. But it is ungrounded: `claims-near.txt` cites no pillar's visible Ten God except the month's.
- **In S:** the same meaning appears in S [2.2] "Sie steht nicht offen oben auf einer Säule", S [5.1] "sie steht auf keiner Säule oben" and S [5.2] "ohne an die Oberfläche zu treten". It is true there: `chart-source.txt` shows the visible Ten Gods as RobWealth, HurtingOfficer, -, IndirectWealth, with no Fire stem.
- **Domain note, not taken from the files:** in N, HurtingOfficer on the month stem and DirectOfficer on the hour stem are both visible. That is the classical 伤官见官 configuration, and N [2.4]'s "Offen steht der Einwand, was ihm entgegensteht, liegt tiefer" states the opposite.

**Additional observation (outside the contract, for the PO):** S [5.5] says "Weder hat die Anforderung eigene Zweige noch die Unterstützung; in deinem Chart teilen sie sich das Innere von Jahreszweig und Monatszweig". But S [5.2] places the requirement in the hour branch too, and S never claims the resource is there. As written, the two paragraphs contradict each other. On the chart the statement happens to be true, because the hour branch holds Ji (IndirectRes), a fact S never claims.
