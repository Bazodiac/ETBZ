1. **`STOCK_PARAGRAPH_REUSE` — BLOCKING — S [6.3] ↔ N [2.7]**, with the same thesis in N's chapter-3 title and echoed in N [5.6].
   - **What recurs:** the same claim identities, and a thesis carried by no claim: the expression is what shows outside, and the demand runs only beneath. The isomorphic scene ends in "darunter … mitläuft" in both.
   - **Why it fails for N:** both readings define the demand by its relation to the day master (N [2.0] "Seine Phase ist diejenige, die den Tagesmeister begrenzt"; N [5.3] "Sie ist die Phase, die ihn begrenzt"). N's hour stem is Bing (Feuer), visible, DirectOfficer. So the thesis holds for S and fails for N.
   - **The reading-level choice:** under the narrow reading — Seven Killing as a label only — the statement holds for both charts and this pair would pass. I did not adopt that reading, because the claims cite exactly the relation (`controls_day_master`), and the readings define the voice the same way.
   - **The signature:** the hour pillar is the only input that differs between S and N, yet the thesis did not change. P12 is the control case: an equivalent uncited absence statement there holds for both charts.
   - **Domain facts used:** that DirectOfficer is in the same family as Seven Killing, and that Ding is Fire, are standard BaZi facts. The judge files show them only indirectly, through S [2.1] "Familie von Struktur und Anforderung" and N [2.1] "kein geregeltes, abgefedertes Regelwerk".
